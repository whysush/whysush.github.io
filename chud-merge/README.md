# Chud Merge

A hand-drawn slide-and-merge game in the style of Juices Merge.

- Tap (or press and slide your finger) to place the head across the table, then let go: it shoots straight forward, slides with friction, and bounces off the rails and other heads.
- Two heads of the same tier merge into the next tier: Soylet → Intern Soy → Gasper → Consoomer → Discord Mod → Reddit Admin → Basement Lord → Final Boss → Ascended.
- After every shot, once the table settles, any head resting behind the red dashed line ends the run.
- Merge two Ascended to win.
- Ban Hammer: removes one head of your choice. You start with one and earn another every 2000 points (max 3).
- Keyboard: ←/→ to move, Space or ↑ to shoot.

Play: https://whysush.github.io/chud-merge/
